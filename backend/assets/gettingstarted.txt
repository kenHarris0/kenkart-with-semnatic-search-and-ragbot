# Ken Kart – Getting Started Guide

This guide walks a new user through setting up Ken Kart and placing their first order. Use this as a reference for onboarding-related support questions.

## Step 1: Download & Install

Download Ken Kart from the Google Play Store (Android) or the App Store (iOS), or visit www.kenkart.app on desktop/mobile browser. Search "Ken Kart" and look for the official app icon.

## Step 2: Create Your Account

1. Open the app and tap "Sign Up."
2. Enter your mobile number or email address.
3. Enter the OTP sent to you within 5 minutes, or click the verification link if you signed up with email.
4. Set a password and optionally enable biometric login (fingerprint/Face ID) on mobile for faster access next time.

## Step 3: Complete Your Profile

1. Enter your name and (optionally) a profile photo.
2. Add your delivery address(es) — you can save multiple (Home, Work, etc.) and pick a default.
3. Set your communication preferences (order updates via SMS, email, and/or push notification).

## Step 4: Add a Payment Method (Optional at This Stage)

1. Go to Account → Payments → Add Payment Method.
2. Choose card, UPI, net banking, or wallet top-up.
3. You can also choose Cash on Delivery (COD) at checkout for eligible orders instead of saving a payment method now.
4. Payment is only required/collected at checkout, not at sign-up.

## Step 5: Browse and Find Products

1. Use the search bar to look for specific products, or browse by category from the home screen (Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen, Groceries, etc.).
2. Use filters (price, brand, rating, delivery speed, "Ken Kart Assured") to narrow results.
3. Tap a product to view details, images, specifications, seller info, and customer reviews.

## Step 6: Add to Cart and Checkout

1. Tap "Add to Cart" on the product page, or "Buy Now" to go straight to checkout.
2. Review your cart — adjust quantities or remove items as needed.
3. Apply a coupon code if you have one.
4. Select a delivery address (or add a new one).
5. Choose a delivery speed if multiple options are shown (Standard, Express, etc.).
6. Choose a payment method and confirm the order.

## Step 7: Track Your Order

1. Go to Account → My Orders to see all current and past orders.
2. Tap an order to view its live tracking status: Confirmed → Packed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered.
3. Enable push notifications to get real-time updates at each stage.

## Step 8: After Delivery

1. Once delivered, you can rate and review the product from the order page.
2. If there's an issue with the item (damaged, wrong, not as described), use "Report an Issue" on the order within the applicable return window (see Policies document).
3. Your invoice is available for download from the order page at any time.

## Tips for New Users

- Add items to your "Wishlist" (heart icon) to save them for later without adding to cart.
- Turn on "Notify Me" for out-of-stock items you want.
- Check the seller rating and "Ken Kart Assured" badge before buying from a new seller.
- Use the Ken Kart Wallet to get faster checkout and occasional cashback offers.
